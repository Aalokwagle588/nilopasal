Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after my 2-day free trial ends?

After 48 hours, the wholesale product cost (`DSN product cost`) will be masked/blurred in your orders and catalog views. You can continue browsing, but you will need to subscribe to Rs. 2,500/month to unlock cost prices and calculate net profit margins.

Why does Nilopasal charge Rs. 2,500 per month for cost price access?

Charging a monthly subscription protects wholesale supplier rates from public exposure and ensures that only serious, verified resellers gain access to supplier pricing, bulk order rates, and dedicated logistics support.

Which payment gateways are supported for subscription renewal?

We support instant payments through eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay QR, and Direct Bank Transfer to Nilopasal platform accounts.
