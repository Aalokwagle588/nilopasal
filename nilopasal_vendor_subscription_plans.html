Understanding SKUs vs Products

How Nilopasal counts variants in subscription plans

A Parent Product is the overall item line (e.g. "Cotton Crewneck T-Shirt"). Each specific size, color, or style combination is an SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) with its own inventory level.

Example: 1 Parent T-shirt Listing 6 SKUs Total
Black / Medium
Black / Large
Black / XL
White / Medium
White / Large
White / XL
Pro-Tip: Out-of-stock items marked as "Archived" in your dashboard do not count towards your plan's active SKU quota.
